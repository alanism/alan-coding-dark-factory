# ACDF Hero Cards

These are the individual design and engineering Hero Lens cards used during Stage 3 review and, when selected by the user, Council-led decision votes.

- [Engineering Council](Engineering_Council/) — coding, architecture, security, verification, and runtime lenses.
- [Design Council](Design_Council/) — interaction, visual, and product-design lenses.

Cards are advisory evidence. They may recommend, challenge, or vote on an in-scope decision, but they cannot waive lifecycle gates, expand authority, or override hard-stop decisions.
